# ASCII Confirmation Generator Microservice

Display ASCII artwork as confirmation notifications in the terminal.

Input: 1-line string of desired confirmation notification

Example: 
```
Added a song to this playlist.
```

Output: multi-line string of ASCII art that contains the desired confirmation notification

Example: 
```
                  ____                              _
                 / ___| _   _  ___ ___ ___  ___ ___| |
                 \___ \| | | |/ __/ __/ _ \/ __/ __| |
                  ___) | |_| | (_| (_|  __/\__ \__ \_|
                 |____/ \__,_|\___\___\___||___/___(_)

* * * * * * * * * * * * * * * * * * * * * * * * * * * * * * * * * * * *

The following operation completed successfully:

Added a song to this playlist.

* * * * * * * * * * * * * * * * * * * * * * * * * * * * * * * * * * * *

```
